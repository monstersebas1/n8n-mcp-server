How it works

Three steps. Zero friction.

Connect once. Talk naturally. Your AI handles the rest.

STEP 01

Connect to Claude or Cursor

Add the MCP server to your AI assistant in one config line. Works with Claude Desktop, Cursor, Windsurf, and any MCP-compatible tool.

STEP 02

Describe your automation

"Send me a Slack alert when a new HubSpot lead comes in." That's it. No node names, no API keys in your prompt, no manual wiring.

STEP 03

Workflow deploys to n8n

The server builds the workflow, connects the nodes, activates it, and reports back — running automation in seconds.
